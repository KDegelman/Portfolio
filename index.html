About Me

I am particularly interested in tasks that provide me the ability to apply my analytical problem-solving to real technical issues and to continue developing my skills in a professional environment.

Projects

BTB Heavy Equipment — PC Hardware Proposal

Course: Hardware & Technical Communications

Role: Technical Lead

Situation

BTB Heavy Equipment, a fictional client, required 250 desktop computers with a maximum budget of CAD $250,000. Some of the client's original hardware requirements were outdated or restrictive.

Task

As technical lead, I worked with my team to develop a cost-effective computer configuration that met the client's operational requirements.

Actions

Our team evaluated component compatibility, cost, performance, and suitability for office use. We identified problematic requirements and negotiated amendments with the client. We then developed a detailed technical proposal documenting our recommendations.

Results

Our proposed configuration cost CAD $241,912.50 for 250 computers, remaining within budget. Our proposal received the highest rating in the class and won the simulated contract.
